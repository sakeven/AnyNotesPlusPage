# Live voice conversation

Use the voice entry in AI Chat for a two-way conversation. Voice carries over the chat context and available tools, with captions, mute, and end controls. Actions that need review still show a confirmation. Ending voice preserves received conversation history and stops unfinished requests.

{% include guide-screens.html group='voice' %}

1. Open a chat from Home, a note or a Topic, then choose its live voice control.
2. Speak naturally. Captions keep the exchange readable, and you can interrupt an AI reply.
3. Use Mute to silence your microphone or Pause to stop audio temporarily; Resume continues the conversation.
4. End voice and confirm when you are finished. The conversation history remains available.

**Try saying:**

> Let's continue this plan. Find the two least certain assumptions, then ask me about them one at a time.

Recording captures your own speech as a note; live voice is a conversation with AI. To keep the discussion as a note, use the chat's save-note action and confirm. Chat history and attachments stay on this device; the saved note participates in note sync.
