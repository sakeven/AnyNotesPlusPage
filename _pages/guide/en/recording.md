# Recording & live notes

Use this for discussions, interviews, learning, or talking through your own ideas.

{% include guide-screens.html group='recording' %}

1. Choose **Record**. Start inside a Topic when the material belongs to that project.
2. In API-key mode, switch between **Live notes** and **Transcript** to follow key points, open questions, and next steps.
3. Ask for guidance with **Help me think**. Suggestions stay separate from your original speech; live notes are provisional.
4. If optional actions appear, choose **Review details**. Recording pauses so you can edit the title, dates, alerts, or app action before confirming execution.
5. Resume recording manually or choose **Done** to save. The complete recording is still transcribed and organized after saving.

**Try this after saving:**

> List the decisions, unresolved questions, and actions from this discussion. Include owners only where someone was explicitly named.

Live insights currently require an API key. Codex sign-in still supports recording and transcription after saving. If the live connection drops, reconnect on the recording screen and check transcription progress. Suggestions and finishing a recording do not execute actions automatically; cancelling a recording does not undo external actions you already confirmed.
