# AI text chat

Start an independent chat from home, or choose **Explore further** in a note or Topic to include its context. Add attachments, edit sent questions, stop responses, and resume earlier conversations from local history.

{% include guide-screens.html group='chat' %}

Recording captures your own speech as a note; live voice is a conversation with AI. To keep the discussion as a note, use the chat's save-note action and confirm. Chat history and attachments stay on this device; the saved note participates in note sync.
