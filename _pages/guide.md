# Getting started

1. Open AnyNotes+, choose **Note**, write a thought, and save it.
2. In **Settings > AI Service**, enter your OpenAI API key or sign in with Codex. AI features on iPhone and iPad also require AnyNotes Pro.
3. Read the organized title, summary, result, and next steps. Saving the original and AI processing are separate; failed processing can be retried later.
4. Choose **Explore further** to continue a conversation with the note as context.
5. Add related material to a Topic so you can develop it together.

| Platform or feature | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone / iPad AI features | AnyNotes Pro and a working API key or Codex sign-in |
| Mac | No AnyNotes Pro requirement; an AI account is still needed |
| Live recording insights | iOS, API-key mode, and a service and network that support live transcription |
| Calendar, reminders, photos, health, and location tools | iOS with permission for each source; alarms and timers require iOS 26 or later |

AnyNotes Pro and AI service usage are separate. Free TestFlight subscriptions do not make your configured AI service usage free. Models and some capabilities depend on your account and service.
